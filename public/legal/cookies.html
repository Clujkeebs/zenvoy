What we store on your device

  • Sign-in session. Supabase keeps a session so you stay logged in. This is required for the app to work.
  • zv_cookie_choice. Your Accept or Essential only choice. Essential.
  • zv_pending_ref. A referral code typed before the magic link is clicked, then removed. Essential to that feature.

Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics, if enabled on the host, does not use a cookie. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you choose Accept and the site is built with a measurement ID. You can clear the choice by deleting site data for zenvylo.app in your browser.

Proof links

A proof page is stored in the link (the part after #). It is not a cookie. It is not sent to our server unless you are signed in and the optional save succeeds.

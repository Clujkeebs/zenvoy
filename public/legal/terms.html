The service

Zenvylo helps freelancers find local businesses and look at their public websites. It reads business listings from OpenStreetMap and fetches publicly available web pages. It records things it can see, such as whether a page loaded, whether it used HTTPS, and whether certain tags or links were present. It can store those results, compare a later fetch, and build a page you can share.

Some plans can ask a language model to draft emails or other text. That text is generated. It is not a fact, a promise, or professional advice. You have to read it before you send it.

No guarantees

We do not guarantee that a scan will find customers, that a measurement is complete, that a website will look the same when the owner checks it, or that anyone will reply to you. Scores are a ranking of the checks we ran, not a prediction of revenue or traffic. OpenStreetMap data can be missing or wrong. A fetch can fail because a site blocks automated requests.
